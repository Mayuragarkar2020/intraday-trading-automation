Replay assumptions and current limits

₹9.80 lakh capital · 1% planned risk including estimated costs · One entry per session · Long only · No leverage. The current trade manager requests cost breakeven after 1.5R and an exit after an EMA10 close.

Default test windows are 09:45–11:30 IST for entries and 15:15 for a flatten request. Windows, costs, slippage and volume participation are recorded assumptions. A flatten request does not guarantee a fill. Gap losses can exceed planned risk.

A signal must precede its first replay bar. Ambiguous entry/stop bars are rejected; stop changes become effective on the next bar; EMA exits use the next opening price. Partial entry remainders are cancelled. Missing bars block the run; an unfilled exit stays open.

Current scope: one normal 2026 session, up to 25 explicit plans and 75 bars per plan. Reference status and EMA values are declared by the input, not verified by this lab. Live-feed capture, broker reconciliation and automatic scanner-to-paper handoff remain pending.
